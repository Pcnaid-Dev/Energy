What We Do

End-to-end energy procurement, audited.

Customized energy solutions for businesses and residential customers — competitive electricity and gas plans, energy consulting, and reliable support to optimize costs and improve efficiency.

01 · Efficiency

Reducing usage, driving efficiency.

We deliver custom energy solutions that help our clients become more energy-efficient and eco-friendly — from supplier selection to on-site optimization.

02 · Services

How procurement actually works.

  1. Analyze your current usage from a single recent bill.
  2. Scour the supplier market for best available pricing and products.
  3. Compare products, pricing, terms, and conditions side-by-side.
  4. Recommend the attribute mix that fits your needs.
  5. Oversee paperwork and the transition to your new supplier.
03 · Rate Analysis

Complimentary rate analysis.

We accurately assess your energy spend and surface significant savings by scanning 90+ suppliers across every deregulated market — then we procure the supplier, product, and rate that best fits your operation.

04 · Markets

Deregulated energy markets.

In deregulated markets — most of Texas plus parts of Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and several others — homes and businesses can shop and switch suppliers. Competition lowers prices and improves service: we make that comparison effortless.

05 · Audits

Accuracy with utility bill audits.

We audit electric and gas utility bills to identify billing discrepancies and opportunities for refunds — a frequent source of recovered cash for commercial customers.

06 · Suppliers

100+ energy supplier partners.

Our job is the best price on your energy — saving you thousands every year. With Pcnaid you get neutral access to top suppliers, without the per-supplier sales call.
